The remainder of this thesis is organized as follows.
Chapter~\ref{chapBackground} provides background on fire blight biology,
relevant sensing modalities, 3D semantic mapping, and prior work on
agricultural robotics and NBV planning.

multi-modal dataset, and 2D detection models.
Chapter~\ref{chapNBV} presents the semantic OctoMap representation and
the proposed NBV planners.
Chapter~\ref{chapErwin} describes the full integrated system and reports
field validation results.
Chapter~\ref{chapConclusions} concludes the thesis and discusses
directions for future work.
